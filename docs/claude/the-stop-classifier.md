# The stop classifier (the model's side of stop verdicts)

**Read this when** you change `core/attention.go`, the trigger in `deliverHookScan`, `InstanceView.Verdict*` or `NeedsYou`, or how the TUI and the PWA alert on a stopped turn.

**What it is.** A Claude session that wants a decision often ends its turn saying so, with no dialog. For each parent `Stop` that leaves Claude Ready, the model has a headless Haiku read the turn's last message and give a verdict: `needs_you`, `done`, `waiting` or `stopped` (the `attention` package: the verdicts, the settings block, the request, the prompt, the answer check and the call log). `InstanceView.NeedsYou()` is `Prompting` or a Ready session's `needs_you`, and every alert reads it. Design: [`../superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-stop-verdicts-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-stop-verdicts-design.md); plan and its amendments: [`../superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-stop-verdicts.md`](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-stop-verdicts.md).

## Rules

1. **A verdict applies only to the Stop it judged.** The session names its last message by the Stop's time (`Instance.LastStop`); the model keys calls and verdicts by `(InstanceID, Stop time)` and applies or shows one only while that Stop is the current one with its message still current (`deliverVerdict`, `verdictOf`). A prompt, a permission request, a newer Stop or a relaunch ends it. *Silent:* a late answer raises "asks you" on a session the person already answered. Enforced by `TestAttention_AStaleAnswerIsDropped` and `TestAttention_AStopGetsItsVerdict` (a reply ends the verdict).
2. **Queue a call only from `deliverHookScan`, for a new parent Stop that left Claude Ready with a non-empty message, outside a workspace terminal, and at most one per session.** A Stop with a running subagent resolves to Running and asks nothing; a newer Stop replaces the session's waiting call (`queueVerdict`). *Silent:* a call per scan bills one turn many times. Enforced by `TestAttention_WhatQueuesNothing` (both sides) and `TestAttention_ANewerStopReplacesAWaitingCall`.
3. **Fail closed: a skip, a timeout or a bad answer leaves no verdict, never a guessed one.** `attentionOff` (no global dir, a bad block, `attention.enabled` false, a pause) queues nothing; `verdictInput` skips an unregistered account and one at `advisorUsageLimit` of its 5-hour window; a call that waited past `wait_seconds` for a slot is skipped on the tick. Each skip and failure clears `VerdictPending`, so the push falls back to finished. Three failures in a row pause the classifier for `attentionPauseFor` with one Notice. Enforced by `TestAttention_AHighUsageAccountIsSkipped`, `TestAttention_ACallThatWaitedTooLongIsSkipped` and `TestAttention_ThreeFailuresPauseWithOneNotice`.
4. **Each call runs as its session's own account** (`accountEnv(inst.Account())`), with the session's program, no tools (`advisor.Call.NoTools`) and the advisor's lockdown, in the empty `<globalDir>/attention/run/`. *Silent:* a work session's text sent through a personal account. Enforced by `TestAttention_EachCallRunsAsItsSessionsAccount`.
5. **A restart re-judges from the call log, not the CLI.** A cold hook scan replays every session's last Stop, so the boot queues a call per stopped session; `bootAttention` loads the log's answers (`cacheOf`) and `queueVerdict` takes a cached one with no call. On the web side `pushWatch` never pushes for a turn that ended before its workspace's baseline (`since`). Enforced by `TestAttention_TheCallLogAnswersAReplayedStop` and `TestPushWatch_ATurnEndedBeforeTheBaselineNeverPushes`.

## How it fits

- **The alerts:** `ui/card.go` (`NeedsAttention`, `asksYou`, the Ready label `❯ asks: …`, `✓ done`, `◷ waiting`, `overviewTier`), `app/overview.go` (the jump and the peer counts), `app/workspaces.go` (`tabStatusOf`), `internal/web/fleet.go` (`groupOf`: "Asks you", "Done", "Waiting"; `sessionJSON`'s `verdict` and `gist`), `internal/web/triggers.go` (`verdictPush`) and `static/js/needs.mjs` (the gist as the needs-you box's reason).
- **The pushes:** a Running→Ready edge with `VerdictPending` owes a push; each turn's verdict (`VerdictStop`) pushes once: `needs_you` as waiting, `done` as the `done` kind (under the device's finished toggle), `stopped` or no verdict as finished only when owed, `waiting` none; leaving Ready drops what is owed.
- **The call log** (`<globalDir>/attention/calls.jsonl`) holds calls, never the message or the prompt; one that fails to load is left alone and calls are kept in memory.
- **Tests:** `atModel` (`core/attention_test.go`) boots the classifier under a fresh global dir with a session on launch L1; `atClassifier` points it at a fake `claude` (`attentionState.program`) that saves each stdin and `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`; `atStop` and `atPrompt` deliver hook scans; `atRun` runs the calls to the end. `core.ViewForTest` builds its bare model with `newAttentionState()`, or `verdictOf` dereferences nil.
- **Not done:** `core/CLAUDE.md` is at its byte budget, so these rules live here; a rule there would point to this guide.
