# Web API

Generated by `go generate ./internal/web` from the route table (`internal/web/routes.go`), the stream's events table (`internal/web/stream.go`) and the union types (`internal/web/unions.go`); do not edit it by hand. Every route needs the paired device's cookie except the one marked open. `internal/web/ui/src/api/gen.ts` is the app's typed form of this reference, and `internal/web/api.fields` pins every field, route, event and union value that has shipped: one may be added, and removing or changing one means deleting its line there by hand in the same commit.

## Routes

### `POST /api/v1/sessions/{id}/send`

`{text}`: sends the text to the session through its loom mod as the person's own words (`core.OriginWeb`); a reply that is an allowed slash command runs it, and its result arrives as a notice. The text is 1 byte to 64 KiB after trimming, else 400.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `send`
- Body: `SendBody`
- Response: `Notices`

### `POST /api/v1/sessions/{id}/merge`

`{source}`: merges the session `source` names into the session in the URL, as the TUI's picker does. A `source` that is no session ID is 400, one that names no session 404, and a merge the model refuses 409 with its words.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `merge`
- Body: `MergeBody`
- Response: `Notices`

### `POST /api/v1/sessions/{id}/pause`

The pause request of the session: 200 with its notices, 409 with the model's own words when it refuses, and 202 `{"pending": true}` when it is still running after 30 s, its outcome then arriving on the stream.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `pause`
- Response: `Notices`

### `POST /api/v1/sessions/{id}/resume`

The resume request of the session: 200 with its notices, 409 with the model's own words when it refuses, and 202 `{"pending": true}` when it is still running after 30 s, its outcome then arriving on the stream.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `resume`
- Response: `Notices`

### `POST /api/v1/sessions/{id}/kill`

### `GET /api/v1/sessions/{id}/message`

The session's last message as a tree of plain nodes (`markdown.go`) with the text it was built from: `{text, blocks, truncated}`. No screen draws it now, and the route stays. A workspace terminal is no session of the app's: 404.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `message`
- Response: `Message`

### `GET /api/v1/sessions/{id}/turns`

A page of the session's conversation: `{conversation, turns, earlier, reset, skipped}` and, on a subagent's page, `agent`. `before` (the page before, from a page's `earlier`) and `after` (the turns from a turn's `id` on) are byte offsets, at most one set, else 400; `agent` reads that subagent's own transcript in place of the conversation, and an id Claude could not have written is 400. A text item crosses as its tree, never as its text; a page holds at most 10 turns and, past its newest, 256 KiB of text.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `turns`
- Query: `TurnsParams`
- Response: `Turns`

### `GET /api/v1/sessions/{id}/diff`

The session's worktree diff against its base commit: `{base, files}`, a file `{path, old_path, status, binary, added, removed, hunks, omitted}`. A diff keeps at most 1 MiB of lines, 5,000 lines a file and 2,000 bytes a line; `omitted` is `lines` or `budget` when a file's hunks were dropped.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `diff`
- Response: `Patch`

### `GET /api/v1/sessions/{id}/jobs/{job}/log`

A job of the session's output, from the byte offset `after` or, when `after` is absent, its tail: `{text, start, end, cut, reset}`, at most 64 KiB, cleaned for display. An `after` past the file's end answers a fresh tail with `reset`. A malformed `job` or `after` is 400; a job that is not the session's, or that the queue no longer holds, is the model's 409.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `jobLog`
- Query: `JobLogParams`
- Response: `JobLog`

### `GET /api/v1/sessions/{id}/flow`

The board of the flow whose plan (or spec, in design) is `plan` in the session's worktree: `{summary, stages, base, start, end, premise, stories, final, question, kept, others, costs}`. No `plan` is 400, and a session with no worktree the model's 409.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `flow`
- Query: `FlowParams`
- Response: `Flow`

### `GET /api/v1/sessions/{id}/flow/doc`

One of the flow's documents as its tree, `{path, blocks, cut}`, each heading with its source `line`; at most 1 MiB and 40,000 nodes. `doc` is `spec`, `skeleton`, `plan`, `record`, `drafts-state` or `report:<agent run>`, and the model refuses any other with 409; a missing `plan` or `doc` is 400.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `flowDoc`
- Query: `FlowDocParams`
- Response: `FlowDoc`

### `GET /api/v1/flows`

Every session's flows and the host's past runs: `{live, past}`, a live one `{session, title, flow}`, a past one `{key, file, stem, plan, stories, ended, end, usd, unreadable}`, newest first.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `flows`
- Response: `Flows`

### `POST /api/v1/sessions/{id}/jobs/{job}/{action}`

A control of one of the session's jobs (the job controls spec): `action` is `start` (start a queued job now, past the queue's fit), `park` or `unpark` (a waiting job), `pause` or `resume` (a running one). Any other action is 404 (`no such action`), a malformed `job` 400, and a refusal, a job that is not the session's included, the model's 409.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `jobControl`
- Response: `Notices`

### `POST /api/v1/sessions/{id}/asks/{ask}/answer`

`{answers}` (each question's `labels`, `other` and `note`, by its text), `{chat, chat_text}`, or `{allow, suggestion}` / `{allow: false, message}`: answers one of the session's asks. 200 once the session's loom mod took the answer, 409 with the model's words for every refusal; an `ask` ID over 128 bytes, more than 8 answers or 8 labels a question, or a text over 64 KiB is 400.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `answer`
- Body: `AnswerBody`
- Response: `Notices`

### `POST /api/v1/sessions`

`{workspace, title, prompt}` and the optional `account`, `model`, `permission_mode`, `effort`: creates and starts a session as `loom session new` does; 201 `{id, notices}`. The prompt is at most 64 KiB and the title as `core.CheckNewTitle` says; an override that is no registered account, no model in `config.ClaudeModelAliases()`, no mode in `config.ClaudePermissionModes` or no effort in `config.ClaudeEfforts` is 400, and a workspace that is full or read-only, a title the workspace holds, or remote control the account's login can't use is 409.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 201
- Client: `create`
- Body: `CreateBody`
- Response: `Created`

### `GET /api/v1/launch-defaults`

`?workspace=<id>`: what the new-session sheet pre-fills (`account`, `model`, `permission_mode`, `effort`) and the choices it offers (`accounts`, `models`, `permission_modes`, `efforts`), from the account registry, `config.ClaudeModelAliases()`, `config.ClaudePermissionModes` and `config.ClaudeEfforts`, so the app hard-codes none. An unknown workspace is 400.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `launchDefaults`
- Query: `LaunchDefaultsParams`
- Response: `LaunchDefaults`

### `PUT /api/v1/device`

`{waiting, finished, pause}`: this device's notification settings, each field optional and a missing one left as it is; `pause` is `"1h"` (until an hour from now) or `"none"` (ends a pause), any other value is 400. Answers the device as the model stored it.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `putDevice`
- Body: `DeviceBody`
- Response: `Device`

### `PUT /api/v1/quick-replies`

`{replies}`: the quick replies every paired phone offers above its reply box, or `null` (or no field) for the defaults; a list `core.NormalizeQuickReplies` refuses is 400 with its words. Answers `{quick_replies}`, the list as the app reads it.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `putQuickReplies`
- Body: `QuickRepliesBody`
- Response: `QuickReplies`

### `POST /api/v1/device/push`

The browser's `PushSubscription.toJSON()`: stores this device's push subscription and answers the device. An endpoint that is not `https`, over 2 KiB, or not on `web.push.apple.com`, `fcm.googleapis.com`, `updates.push.services.mozilla.com` or a host ending in `.notify.windows.com`, with a user or a port but 443, or keys of the wrong size, is 400.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `postPush`
- Body: `SubscriptionBody`
- Response: `Device`

### `DELETE /api/v1/device/push`

Drops this phone's push subscription, which the model records as expired; the app asks for it when it finds on a launch that the phone holds none or may not notify. 204.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 204
- Client: `deletePush`

### `DELETE /api/v1/device`

Unpairs this phone: the model forgets the device, which ends its streams and drops its subscription, and the cookie is cleared. 204, a device already gone included.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 204
- Client: `deleteDevice`

### `POST /api/v1/pair`

`{code}`: trades a pairing code for the device cookie and answers `{"device": "<name>"}`. The one route served without a cookie. A wrong or used code is 400, too many wrong ones 429, a name taken or 16 devices paired 409, the web off 503, and any other failure a fixed 500.

- Auth: none (open)
- Success: 200
- Client: `pair`
- Body: `PairBody`
- Response: `Pair`

### `GET /api/v1/push-key`

The VAPID public key the app subscribes with, as unpadded base64url: `{key}`. 503 when push is not available.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Success: 200
- Client: `pushKey`
- Response: `PushKey`

### `GET /api/v1/sessions/{id}/mockup`

The page the session's brainstorm companion serves now, as HTML under `mockupCSP` with `Cache-Control: no-store`, for the app's iframe. A refusal (409: no companion, or a page refused) or a timeout (504) is a small HTML page that says why, under the same policy.

- Auth: the device cookie
- Page: HTML for the app's iframe; the generated client builds its URL as `mockupURL`.
