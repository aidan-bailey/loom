# session/vt

The embedded terminal emulator behind every pane: the `Emulator` interface (`vt.go`), its implementation over the vendored `github.com/charmbracelet/x/vt` (`xvt.go`), and the alt-screen filter the output pump writes through (`altfilter.go`). Facts in the `vt` section of [`../README.md`](../README.md).

## Rules when modifying this package

- **In x/vt `Callbacks`, only assign wrapper fields; notify after the write lock is released.** Callbacks fire inside `emu.Write` under the wrapper's write lock: re-locking `e.mu` deadlocks (an RWMutex is not reentrant), and calling app code there runs it under the lock. To notify, set a pending flag in the callback and have `Write`/`Resize` invoke the handler after unlocking, as the bell does; expose stored state through a read-locked accessor. A bell handler once called `tea.Program.Send` under the lock and froze the whole UI. **Enforced** for the bell by `TestBell_FiresOutsideWriteLock` and for query replies by `TestXVT_QueryReplyDoesNotBlock`; a new callback needs its own. Diagnosis: [`../../docs/claude/debugging-freezes.md`](../../docs/claude/debugging-freezes.md)
- **Keep `vt.NewAltScreenFilter` between the output pump and the emulator.** tmux attach clients sit on the alternate screen for good, and x/vt keeps scrollback only on the primary screen, so without the filter scroll-back silently stops accumulating (zero lines, no error). **Enforced** by `TestScrollbackAccumulation_RealTmux` and the `TestAltScreenFilter_…` tests.
- **Keep `TmuxSession.PaneTitle` rejecting invalid UTF-8, and the wrapper negating x/vt's `CursorStyle` blink argument, until upstream fixes them.** The vendored OSC-title parser truncates on the lead byte of a multi-byte sequence, and `CursorStyle` delivers `!blink` under a parameter named `blink`. Both pins are written to fail informatively once upstream changes, so re-run them whenever the x/vt version in `go.mod` moves, and simplify the guard when the title pin starts passing. **Enforced** by `TestTitle_NonASCIITruncatesInVendoredParser` and `TestCursor_DECSCUSRShapes`, and `TestPaneTitle_RejectsInvalidUTF8` in `session/tmux`.
- **Pace the output of a real-tmux test that asserts scroll delivery.** tmux repaints on its own flushes, and more than a screenful between two flushes (a loaded machine, the Nix build) arrives as one clear-and-redraw, so at most one screen reaches scrollback ("got exactly 10" on a 10-row pane is the signature); emit line by line with a short sleep and gate later phases on the last line reaching the screen. To reproduce a loaded CI run, saturate every core with a busy loop while the test runs. **Convention** — a test that passes locally and fails in the Nix sandbox.
- **Let `TestSyncOutputDefeatsEmulatorScrollback_RealTmux` skip below tmux 3.7.** Older tmux still scrolls attach clients through synchronized updates, so the pin has nothing to show there, and CI's tmux comes from its runner image. **Convention** — a test that fails on every machine with an older tmux.
